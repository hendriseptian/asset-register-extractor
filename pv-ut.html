01 · SOURCE IDENTIFICATION

Automatic Equipment Matching

No need to select equipment. Gemini reads the Equipment Tag from the UT report and links the readings to the matching PV record.

If the same Equipment Tag already exists, UT readings are added to that equipment in Data Review. A new tag creates a draft equipment record. If the tag is missing or unclear, the readings are sent to Pending Identification for manual verification.
